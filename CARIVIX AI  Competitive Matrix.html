CARIVIX AI Competitive Matrix

How 15 competitors and benchmarks score across eight capabilities, with CARIVIX's own position shown as implemented or proposed instead of scored.

Source: Competitive Intelligence Report v1.0, 5 Oct 2026 · Internal / Confidential · Research Draft
Scores are qualitative research assessments of how central and mature each capability is in publicly documented products, not benchmark results. CARIVIX is not scored because most of its architecture is proposed. Only the Knowledge/RAG module has been implemented and tested.

Positions use the report's qualitative 0–5 scores. Combined axes are simple averages of the capabilities named. Bubbles that share a position are fanned out slightly. Bubble size is the weighted score from the Capability matrix tab, so changing weights there resizes the bubbles. The diamond is a movable what-if marker for CARIVIX, not a score: drag it (or focus it and use the arrow keys) to see which competitors sit nearest.

Weighted score is the weight-averaged mean of the eight capability scores for each competitor. It is a screening aid for comparing overlap with a chosen focus, not a ranking of overall product quality. Click a column header to sort.

Pricing is uneven across vendors, so treat these as indicative signals, not like-for-like comparisons. CARIVIX has no validated pricing model yet; the report proposes enterprise or government licence plus implementation, data/GIS services and optional usage-based AI charges as a hypothesis.
